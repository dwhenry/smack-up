# Crookie's voice

Crookie's lines are recordings in this folder. The games play the file whose name matches what Crookie is saying. Any line that hasn't been recorded yet just shows in his speech bubble with no sound, so you can add recordings a few at a time.

## The voice

A deep, warm, unhurried storyteller's voice that suddenly goes rubbery, goofy and over the top. Friendly and smiley, never scary. Crookie speaks in proper sentences. He is not Cookie Monster: no gravelly “me want cookie”.

## Recording it all in one go

The easiest way is one long recording, read straight down the list. A phone voice memo is fine.

- Leave a gap of **about 2 seconds** between each one. The file gets split up at the gaps.
- If you fluff one, stop, wait **5 seconds or more**, then say it again. A clip followed by a long gap is treated as a mistake and dropped.
- Don't worry about the gaps between words in a line. Short pauses are fine.
- Skip any suggestion you don't like, and say which ones you skipped.

When you've finished, put the recording in this folder (`audio/crookie/`). Any name is fine, and a voice-memo `.m4a` is fine too. Then ask Claude to cut it up. The long recording stays on your computer and never goes on the website. Only the cut-up clips do.

## Adding recordings one at a time

1. Record the line as an **MP3**. A phone voice-memo app is fine. Keep each one short, and trim the silence at the start and end.
2. Name it exactly as in the tables below, all lower case.
3. Put it in this folder (`audio/crookie/`) and push to GitHub. The site picks it up in a minute or two.

## Roars

About one line in three, Crookie lets out a random roar first and then says his line. Record three different short, playful bear roars (happy and rumbly, a second or so each). Any you leave out are skipped.

| File | What to record |
|---|---|
| `roar-1.mp3` | A short, playful bear roar |
| `roar-2.mp3` | A different playful roar |
| `roar-3.mp3` | A happy, rumbly roar-and-chuckle |
