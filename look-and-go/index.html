Grown-up menu

⏏ Exit to all games

The game is paused while this is open. To get here, press and hold “Hold for menu” at the bottom left, or press Esc.

Looking

“Anywhere” is for the very first steps. It counts as looking while the eye tracker is moving the pointer, so the kart stops when the pointer stops (for example when he looks away from the screen).
A bigger number makes it easier. Only used when looking at the kart.

Driving

Seconds of driving before the finish flag comes along with a cheer. 0 turns it off.
