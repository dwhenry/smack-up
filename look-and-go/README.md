# Look and Go

A first eye-gaze game, for the cause-and-effect stage: look, and something happens; look away, and it stops.

Play at **https://smack-up.decoybecoy.com/look-and-go/**

## How it plays

Crookie sits in his go-kart on a cartoon road. While your child looks at the kart, the engine revs, smoke puffs out of the exhaust and the kart zooms along: the road, trees and hills scroll past, the wheels spin and Crookie grins and waves. Looking away makes the engine fade and the kart roll to a stop, and Crookie turns his googly eyes to look back at them.

Crookie yells things as he drives (“Full steam ahead!”, “The cookies are escaping!”), and asks “Why are we slowing down?” when he looks away. Some yells come with something to see, like a cookie whizzing past or cookies rolling off down the road. There's nothing to get wrong. After a set amount of driving a chequered finish flag comes along, with confetti, a cheer and Crookie's arms in the air, and the flag count at the top goes up.

## Grown-up settings

Press and hold “Hold for menu” at the bottom left for about a second, or press Esc. The game pauses while the menu is open, and “Exit to all games” takes you back to the main menu.

- **What makes the kart go:** looking at the kart (default), or looking anywhere on the screen for the very first steps. “Anywhere” counts as looking while the eye tracker is moving the pointer, so the kart stops when the pointer stops, for example when he looks away from the screen.
- **How close to the kart counts:** a bigger number makes it easier (default 90px).
- **Top speed:** slow, medium or fast.
- **Speed:** full speed anywhere on the kart, or faster the closer he looks to the middle. With the second option a soft circle marks the middle (it can be hidden); looking inside it gives full speed and looking further out goes slower.
- **Finish line after this much driving:** seconds of driving before the flag comes (default 20; 0 turns it off).
- **Show where he's looking**, **Engine and sounds** and **Crookie talks**.

Crookie's lines are recordings shared with Crookie's Kitchen (see [audio/crookie](../audio/crookie/README.md) for the script). Any line not recorded yet just shows in his speech bubble. Crookie is inspired by Zak “Crookie” Cruickshank.
