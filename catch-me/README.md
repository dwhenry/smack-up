# Catch Me

A cheeky hamster rolls around in his ball along four paths joined by ladders. Look at him long enough to catch him and he pinballs off the edges of the screen, then lands back on a path, dizzy, and carries on.

Play at **https://smack-up.decoybecoy.com/catch-me/**

The plan, layout and background prompts are in [games/catch-me.md](../games/catch-me.md).

- **Backgrounds:** drawn by the game for now (garden, cupboard, hedge maze). Generated pictures saved here as `garden.jpg`, `cupboard.jpg` or `maze.jpg` replace them automatically; send them over and I'll line the paths up with each one.
- **Voice:** recordings go in [audio/catch-me](../audio/catch-me/README.md), which has the script.
- **Grown-up menu:** press and hold “Hold for menu” at the bottom left, or press Esc. Settings: how long to look to catch him, how close counts, whether he slows down when looked at, running speed, how wild the bounce is, where he plays, the gaze dot, sound and voice.
- **Landscape only:** on a phone held upright it asks you to turn the phone sideways.
