# Look and Go

**Status:** Live at https://smack-up.decoybecoy.com/look-and-go/
**Skill it practises:** Cause and effect, the very first stage: look and it goes, look away and it stops
**Characters:** Crookie in his go-kart

## The idea in one sentence

Look at Crookie's kart and it zooms off with engine noise and smoke; look away and it stops.

## How it plays

The kart sits big in the middle of a cartoon road. While he looks at it, the engine revs, smoke puffs from the exhaust, the scenery scrolls past and Crookie grins and waves. Looking away fades the engine and the kart rolls to a stop, and Crookie turns to look back at him.

With the “faster the closer he looks to the middle” setting, a soft circle marks the middle of the kart: looking inside it gives full speed, looking further out goes slower.

Crookie yells things while driving, and some come with something to see: a cookie flies past for “Did you see that cookie fly past?”, and cookies roll off down the road for “The cookies are escaping!”.

## The reward

The movement and sound themselves, Crookie's yells, and a chequered finish flag with confetti and a cheer after a set amount of driving.

## Grown-up settings

What makes it go (looking at the kart, or anywhere on the screen), how close counts, top speed, full speed anywhere or faster nearer the middle (with the circle on or off), how long until the finish flag, the gaze dot, sound, Crookie talks.

## Ideas and changes

- More levels from the eye-gaze learning path: a green traffic light to look at (targeting), picking a racer from two family photos (choosing), following a slow car (tracking), two wide lanes (control).
