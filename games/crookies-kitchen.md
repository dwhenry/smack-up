# Crookie's Kitchen

**Status:** Live at https://smack-up.decoybecoy.com/crookie/
**Skill it practises:** Targeting (choosing a food), steady looking (stirring), and a simple two-way choice at the end
**Characters:** Crookie, inspired by Zak “Crookie” Cruickshank

## The idea in one sentence

Help Crookie the monster bake cookies by looking at foods to drop them in his bowl, then stirring.

## How it plays

1. **Gather:** up to six big food cards (picture and name). Looking at one fills it up, then it flies into the bowl and Crookie says its name. Four foods fill the bowl.
2. **Stir:** the bowl moves to the middle and looking at it stirs. Looking away pauses without losing progress; after a while the bowl flashes and Crookie calls him back.
3. **Bake:** the bowl turns into a pile of cookies (tinted by what went in). Crookie says “Some for me… and some for you!”, then eats one with an eye roll: “My cookie!”
4. **Choose:** two big cards, Play again or Main menu.

Crookie is animated throughout: googly eyes that follow where he looks, eyebrows, waving, cheering, sneaking cookies.

## The reward

Crookie's reactions and voice, the cookie pile, the batch counter.
