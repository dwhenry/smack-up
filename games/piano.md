# Piano

**Status:** Idea
**Skill it practises:** Cause and effect, then exploring and targeting (look at a key and it plays)
**Characters:** Crookie as the audience? (to decide)

## The idea in one sentence

Look at a piano and play sounds - have the option to set noise type.

## How it plays

A row of big, chunky keys across the screen, each a different colour. Looking at a key presses it down and plays its note, and a little burst of musical notes floats up from it. Moving his eyes along the keys plays a tune up or down the scale. Looking away lets the key spring back up.

I want a row of option for the sound type across the top, each with an image:

- cow (so moos in tone)
- sheep (baa's in tone)
- musical note (so standard tones)
- fart (so fart noises i tone)

In addition I would like a big button the right that plays the intro to seven nation army when clicked (can I do that with licencing?)

## The reward

The notes themselves, coloured notes floating up from each key, and Crookie bopping along. In play-along mode, a cheer and confetti when the tune is finished.

## Grown-up settings

How many keys, how long to look before a key plays, which sound (piano, xylophone \_ no rude sounds)
