# Smack Up

**Status:** Live at https://smack-up.decoybecoy.com/smack-up/
**Skill it practises:** Targeting and steady looking at things that move
**Characters:** Family photos

## The idea in one sentence

Family photos float down on parachutes, and staring at one gets it splatted with something silly.

## How it plays

Photos drift down from the sky on stripy parachutes, swaying gently. Looking at a photo slows it down, makes it wobble and fills a yellow ring around it. When the ring is full, something flies in and hits it: a cream pie, a birthday cake, a tomato, an egg, green slime, a water balloon, a rubber chicken (BONK), a fish (SLAP), or a whoopee zoom where the photo farts and whizzes off like a let-go balloon. The photo then drops off its parachute. Photos he doesn't look at just float away.

Photos come from a shared Google Drive folder that family can add to, or from a folder on the computer. Names in file names (like `Grandma.jpg`) appear under the photo.

## The reward

A big cartoon word (SPLAT!, BONK!, CAKED!), sound effects, a screen shake and a splats counter. Confetti and a cheer every 10 splats.

## Things anyone says

Nothing spoken yet.

## Grown-up settings

How long to look, how close counts, falling speed, photo size, photos at once, names on or off, which splats to use, sound, rude noises, the gaze dot, the Google Drive folder (with a “this visit only” option for shared computers).
