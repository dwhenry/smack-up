Smack Up

Family photos float down. Stare at one and SPLAT, it gets a pie in the face.

Help Crookie the monster bake. Look at the food to put it in the bowl, then stir it into cookies.

Look and Go

Look at Crookie’s kart and it zooms off in a puff of smoke. Look away and it stops. A first eye-gaze game.
